Automatically reads the Instagram session cookie from your browser on this Mac.

Takes 5 seconds and works reliably in Safari and Chrome:

  1. Open instagram.com in Safari and ensure you are logged in.
  2. Press Cmd + Option + C to open the Console, type this command and press Enter:
copy(document.cookie)

3. copy() will instantly copy your Instagram cookies to your macOS clipboard. Paste them in the box below and click Save Session.

Privacy & Safety

  • 100% Local Storage: Your cookies are stored only on your local machine in the data/sessions/ folder.
  • Password-Free: We recommend using session cookies rather than typing raw passwords for maximum security.
  • Safe Rate-Limiting: The downloader includes built-in pauses between requests to protect your account from rate limits.
